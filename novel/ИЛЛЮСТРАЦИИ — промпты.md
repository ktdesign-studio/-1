# Промпты для иллюстраций (Grok)

Стиль серии: «полевой дневник Теи» — будто она сама зарисовывает расследование
между записями. Мистический скетч: тушь + растушёванный графит, сепия, туман.

## Как пользоваться

1. К каждому промпту **спереди подставляйте стилевое ядро** (блок ниже) — так все
   картинки останутся одной серией.
2. Просите соотношение сторон 3:4 (книжная вертикаль) для сцен и 4:3 для разворотов.
3. Модели коверкают кириллицу — поэтому в промптах стоит «illegible handwritten
   notes»: пометки на полях будут выглядеть как почерк, но без читаемых слов.
   Настоящие подписи лучше добавить потом поверх, при вёрстке.
4. Если Grok уходит в «красивое фэнтези» — добавьте в конец:
   `rough unfinished sketch, not a polished painting, visible pencil construction lines`.

## Стилевое ядро (подставлять в начало каждого промпта)

```
Mystical field-journal sketch: ink and smudged charcoal on aged sepia paper,
loose crosshatching, visible pencil construction lines, handwritten illegible
margin notes and small diagram arrows, ink stains and a tea ring on the page,
fog drawn with soft eraser smudges, one single accent of pale ghost-green
ether glow, 19th-century naturalist notebook aesthetic, moody, melancholic,
candlelit atmosphere --no photorealism, no bright colors, no anime
```

## Обложка / фронтиспис

```
[стилевое ядро] + A vertical cross-section sketch of an impossible layered city:
elegant quarters on tall stilts above the clouds, a dense industrial middle city
with gas lamps, and drowned old quarters sinking into thick luminous fog below;
a tiny brass compass drawn in the corner, its needle pointing straight down;
the whole page looks like a cartographer's secret study, aspect 3:4
```

## Глава 1. «Жестяная, шестнадцать»

Сцена: щель в заборе.
```
[стилевое ядро] + A lonely young woman in a worn coat stands at night before
a tall weathered plank fence with rusty barbed wire and an official tin sign;
one plank is pried open leaving a narrow dark gap, thick fog seeps through it
and glows faintly green; unlit street lamps and a ladder leaning against one
lamp post in the background; her hand is in her coat pocket, sketched mid-motion,
aspect 3:4
```

Артефакт: компас Дана (хорошо смотрится как маленькая «вклейка» между главами).
```
[стилевое ядро] + Technical study of an antique brass pocket compass with
an engraved wind rose lid, drawn three times on one page: closed, open, and
in cross-section; the needle drawn tilting impossibly downward through the
page with small annotation arrows; slightly worn, beloved object, aspect 4:3
```

## Глава 2. «Туман, в котором не мокнут»

Сцена: живая улица из мёртвой карты.
```
[стилевое ядро] + A narrow drowned street rising out of waist-deep luminous fog:
crooked old houses with dim greenish windows, laundry lines strung between
them, thin smoke from one chimney; above the rooftops, far away and very high,
a sketched silhouette of a rich city on stilts with perfectly even rows of
warm lights; the page corner holds a small crossed-out map fragment of the
same street, aspect 3:4
```

Сцена: похищение (страшно бережностью, не насилием).
```
[стилевое ядро] + Through a lit window a seated woman is being gently unmade:
her outline unravels into fine threads of pale light pulled toward a cold,
perfectly even beam outside the frame; no monster visible, no violence, the
horror is in the tenderness; heavy fog around the house, a small figure of
a young woman screaming in the street below, mostly in shadow, aspect 3:4
```

## Глава 3. «Сросток»

Эшер, первое появление.
```
[стилевое ядро] + A tall man in a long grey coat half-dissolving into fog:
below the knees his silhouette frays into mist threads, his eyes are the only
ether-green accent on the page; he is drawn twice — once almost human, once
as a loose gesture sketch where the form barely holds; small frightened
margin notes and an arrow labeled with illegible handwriting, aspect 3:4
```

Лавка невозможных вещей.
```
[стилевое ядро] + Interior of a cluttered curiosity shop drawn from a low
armchair viewpoint: shelves of impossible objects (a birdcage holding a tiny
cloud, keys without holes, a bottled lantern flame, maps rolled like scrolls),
one oil lamp burning ether-green; everything sketched in loving detail except
the shop owner, who is only an empty outline standing by the window, aspect 3:4
```

Сросток (схема-диаграмма — для интерлюдий/форзаца).
```
[стилевое ядро] + An anatomical-style diagram of two human silhouettes facing
away from each other, their chest areas connected by an intricate woven knot
of luminous threads with no visible seam; measurement marks, question marks
and crossed-out formulas in illegible handwriting around it, drawn like
a desperate scientific attempt to understand something alive, aspect 4:3
```

## Портретные этюды (для досье)

Тея.
```
[стилевое ядро] + Portrait study of a young woman in her mid-twenties, dark
hair pinned carelessly, tired ironic eyes, ink-stained middle finger of her
right hand drawn as a separate detail study beside the face; she is sketched
as if she doesn't know she is being drawn, looking down at a notebook,
aspect 3:4
```

Дан (для тетрадки Теи — рисунок по памяти).
```
[стилевое ядро] + A loving but unfinished portrait sketch of a nineteen-year-old
boy with a quick grin, drawn from memory — the face is detailed but the edges
trail off unfinished; beside him a careful detailed drawing of the same brass
compass, which is finished completely; one corner of the page is worn from
being touched often, aspect 3:4
```
